Meet the Wonderer

The little stick figure with the big round head is your guide. He's curious, easily surprised, and asks the questions most people keep to themselves.

Always Wondered is for the things you've puzzled over in the shower and never looked up. Every topic passes three tests: a normal person has wondered it, the real answer is surprising, and it will still be true in ten years.
